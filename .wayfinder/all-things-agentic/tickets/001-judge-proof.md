## Question

Which exact product and runtime evidence must CoOps make legible to maximize the current hackathon judging criteria?

## Resolution

Enter Fortified Enterprise Fleet. The binding rules weight Innovation and Operational Utility at 40%, Architectural Discipline and Tech Stack at 30%, and Demo and Production Readiness at 30%. The first judge path should show a non-chat autonomous run, named human authority, a real tool action, runtime identity, failure or blocking behavior, an inspectable trace, and a delivered artifact whose provenance is explicit. Submission proof also needs a clear frontend-backend-Gemini-GCP diagram and visible GCP execution in a video no longer than four minutes.

Sources: [rules](https://allthingsagentichackathon.devpost.com/rules), [resources](https://allthingsagentichackathon.devpost.com/resources), [schedule](https://allthingsagentichackathon.devpost.com/details/dates).
